# Wall Street

One shadow tower. Six desks, **120 staff**, one paper book.

Quote, Research, Risk, Ledger, Control, and The book each have 20 seats. They are staff, not six strategies. The floor does not buy and does not spend desk cash. A check prints skip and leaves the book flat.

Open the Wall Street tower in APEX-CITY. The aisle board is the book. The colored bands are the desks.
