# How to read the city (one page, step 998)

Everything is pretend money.

## The buildings
- A lit tower is open and trading. Its color is its own.
- A black tower with dark windows is closed (its market is shut) or inactive.
- Practice (shadow) towers stay hidden until they make their first trade.
- Research and support buildings never trade. They measure, check and grade.

## The vault
Three lines: the desk book (the vault towers' pretend cash), the practice books (each practice tower's own $500), and all paper together. Losses always count in every line.

## What "counts"
- A practice tower does not count until it has 50 settled results.
- Even after 50, it is promoted only with your OK.
- The scorekeeper grade (promote, keep, pause, retire) is a suggestion only.

## Where to look for answers
| Question | Building |
|---|---|
| Is a tower making money after fees? | Scorekeeper |
| Which league, coin, metal or scan time is losing? | Result splits |
| Are several towers making the same bet? | Risk room |
| Did every building run on time? When is the next run? | Freshness inspector |
| Did a held trade settle right? | Settlement auditor |
| What would skipped trades have made? | What-if lab |
| How often does a 90-cent price really win? | Calibration lab |

## Words
Edge = fair price minus the ask minus the fee. Floor = the smallest edge a tower may buy at; it is locked and never goes lower. Settle = Kalshi pays $1 or $0 per contract.
