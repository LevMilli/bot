# A7. Hourly index tower

## What
Kalshi hourly S&P 500 and Nasdaq-100 markets.

## How often
Every 1 minute, in regular market hours only.

## Fair price
The Nasdaq tower's math (live index plus volatility index) on an hourly clock. Quote must be under 5 minutes old.

## Edge and floor
Index floor 0.02 after fee. That is low, so fees and slippage get extra watching. $25 ticket, max 2 open.

## Avoid stacking
The Risk room shows when this would hold the same bet as Kalshi S&P, Sized Kalshi or Nasdaq. The desk correlation veto must count them as one bet before any practice book opens.

## Steps
1. Log-only for 2 weeks; grade in the what-if lab.
2. Practice book only if positive after fees; 50 results and Levi's OK.

## Risks
Low floor. Tied to three existing towers.
