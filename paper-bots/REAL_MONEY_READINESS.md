# Real money readiness checklist (reference only)

Real money stays off. This list exists so that, if Levi ever wants to talk about it, the
questions are written down first. Nothing here turns anything on. Review it with Levi once
a year.

- [ ] At least 6 months of paper results on the tower, all losses counted.
- [ ] At least 300 settled results with a cautious win rate that clears fees.
- [ ] Settlement auditor: zero unexplained mismatches for 90 days.
- [ ] Pretend fills checked against real order books (strict fill) with slippage measured.
- [ ] Two-person sign-off designed and tested (`LIVE_SWITCH_BUILT` is still `False`).
- [ ] A hard dollar cap per day and per trade, written down and tested.
- [ ] Key storage plan reviewed. Keys never in the repo, never in chat.
- [ ] Legal and tax questions answered by a person, not a bot.
- [ ] Levi's written decision.

Until every box is ticked and Levi decides, the answer is no.
